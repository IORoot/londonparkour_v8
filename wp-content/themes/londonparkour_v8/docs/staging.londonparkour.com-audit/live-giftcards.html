Benefits

Why Choose a Parkour Gift Card?

Memorable Experience
Personal
Each class offers personalized, hands-on instruction from expert coaches in a positive and high-energy environment. Participants of all levels will learn new techniques, build confidence, and have a blast while doing it. Whether they're trying parkour for the first time or mastering advanced moves, every session is designed to be an unforgettable experience.
Flexible and Convenient
Flexible
Our gift cards are fully flexible and can be redeemed at any time, making it easy for participants to fit parkour into their busy lives. There's no need to commit to a fixed schedule—they can use the credit whenever they're ready to jump back into training. Perfect for those who prefer to train on their own terms.
Perfect for Any Occasion
Unique
Parkour Gift Cards make for a unique and thoughtful gift, whether for birthdays, holidays, or just because. Plus, they're shareable! Recipients can split the balance across multiple classes or even share the code with friends or family to join classes together. Give the gift of adventure and fun!

Terms and Conditions.

Classes only
Classes and PTs
These gift cards are only redeemable for classes and personal training sessions only. They cannot be used for any store products, apparel or garments.
Stripe.com
Safe & Secure
All transactions go through our global payment provider Stripe.com. Stripe is certified to the highest compliance standards, with machine learning and fraud detection. Used by Google, Microsoft, Amazon and Spotify, you can be sure your payments will be as secure as possible.
Protected
No Payment Data
LondonParkour does not keep any transactional data or credit card information on our servers. To make things as simple and secure as possible, we directly pass all information through to our payment provider.
365 days
Expiry Date
Every coupon has an expiry date of one year from the date/time of purchase. We endeavour to be as fair as possible, so special circumstances (like a pandemic) will make us much more flexible on this rule.
