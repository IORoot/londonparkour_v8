## Body copy

### Opening

We rebuilt londonparkour.com.

The old site did the job for years: classes, private tuition, gift cards, and a large video library. The new one is the same school — outdoor parkour, coach-led, no membership — with a clearer board, a single price for a standard class, and booking that happens on the page you are already reading.

If you already train with us, nothing about the session itself has changed. The coaches, the sites, and the work on the floor are the same. What changed is how you find a date, pay, and come back.

### What you can do on the new site

- **Book a weekly class** from the agenda. Spaces update live. Sessions are coach-led and capped at twelve.
- **Find the site on a map**, with meeting point and travel notes, then walk from the station.
- **Buy a coupon** — one class, five, or ten — and use it at any of the three sites.
- **Book a private 1:1** against a real slot, with the coach and venue on the card.
- **Open a workshop** as its own date: price, level, film, and a book button for that sitting.
- **Watch the tutorial library** by series, by category, or by a single move — then book the class that teaches it in person.
- **Read the docs**, search the site, or subscribe to the monthly PathFinder note.

The homepage is the short version of all of that: next class, the week’s board, the fare table, private coaching, workshops, the library, the sites, the coaches, then a way onto the list.

- **Evening Intermediate Outdoor**
- **Kids Class West (6–9s)**

Late is normal. The first ten minutes are warm-up. More than twenty minutes behind, message us so we do not hold the spot.

### Private 1:1s

One coach. Just you. From **£65** a session. Shared sessions (two people) are **£40** per person. Blocks of five are available.

This is for:

- **First wall** — you have not trained outdoors before, or not comfortably, and a group is the wrong first room.
- **Comeback** — returning after injury, time off, or a confidence dip.
- **Stuck line** — one movement, spot or technique that will not progress.
- **Pairs** — two people who want focused coaching without a full group.

How you book:

1. Open [/private-coaching/](/private-coaching/) and tap **Book 1:1**.
2. The appointments overlay shows the real slots — date, venue, coach.
3. Confirm and pay. The booking is confirmed instantly. A cancellation policy applies; it is on the page.

You do not fill in a request form and wait. The slot you see is the slot you get.

Teaching since 2005: adults, children, people coming back from injury, people who have never jumped a wall. The session is built around what you need that day. If you do not know yet, the coach will.

### How you book, from any of those pages

Booking is a panel on the page, not a hop through a separate shop.

1. Pick the class, 1:1 slot, workshop date, or coupon pack.
2. The drawer opens with the date, the site, the price, and the waiver.
3. Pay on Stripe’s checkout. Card details never sit on our servers.
4. You land on a confirmation page. The same details arrive by email.

If you already hold a coupon, the code attaches itself. If a class is cancelled, the credit goes back on your account — usually within the hour — and spends on any standard session at any of the three sites. Prefer the money? Reply to the cancellation email.

### How the site was built

This part is for the curious. Skip it if you just want to book.

The new site started as a design file in **Pencil** (Pen.dev): every page, every type size, every hairline, signed off before a line of production markup. That file is the source of truth for copy as well as layout. We did not rewrite the words to sound “more web”.

From Pencil we built a **Storybook** — a living component library in HTML, Tailwind, and daisyUI. Four themes (dark/light × yellow/green), a Swiss grid, sharp corners, parkour movement glyphs, and every block you now see on the homepage: hero, classes, pricing, private coaching, workshops, tutorials, locations, coaches.

Then we ported that library into **WordPress**, class by class, byte for byte. The public site is a classic WordPress theme, not a page-builder collage. Editors compose pages from the same blocks. Classes, coupons, 1:1 slots and workshops are real records in **Class Bookings with Stripe Pro**. Photographs live in the media library and are delivered through **Cloudinary**, so a phone is not asked to download a desktop hero.

**AI was in the room the whole way** — reading the Pencil file, assembling Storybook components, porting them into WordPress, checking contrast and landmarks, wiring booking, maps, search, and the tutorial transcripts. It did not invent the school. The coaches, the syllabus, and the prices are ours. The machine made it possible to hold the design exact from the first frame to the live page.

Under the floor:

- **Stripe** for payment
- **Cloudways** for hosting, with page cache in front of the marketing pages and the booking API kept live
- **OpenStreetMap** for the class map
- **YouTube** for the tutorial players
- **Mailchimp** for the monthly PathFinder dispatch — one email, not a weekly blast

The old gift-card shop is gone. Coupons replace it. Private tuition is no longer a separate product silo; it is a tab next to the weekly classes, with the same booking panel.

### How we keep it up to date

The board you see is the board we teach from.

- **A class date, a cancellation, or a seat count** is edited once, on that class. The agenda, the map, the homepage “next class”, and the book button all read the same record.
- **A workshop** is a dated sitting. When it has run, it moves to past. We do not leave a dead “book now” on a date that has gone.
- **A 1:1 slot** is an appointment. If it is in the overlay, it is bookable.
- **A coupon pack** is a price and a validity window. Changing it on the coupons page changes the fare table on the homepage.
- **A tutorial** is a WordPress post: film, series, category, summary, transcript. New videos join the board; they are not a separate archive.
- **A page photograph** is a WordPress image, served at the size your screen asked for.

If you need to know whether class is running this week, do not screenshot an old timetable. Open [/classes/](/classes/).
