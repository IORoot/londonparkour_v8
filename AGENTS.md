# LondonParkour Google Ads Project

## Business

LondonParkour is a small local business providing parkour and
movement classes in London.

Primary objective:

Generate profitable bookings for LondonParkour classes.

Do not optimise primarily for:
- impressions
- clicks
- CTR
- traffic
- engagement

The ultimate business outcome is paid class bookings.

## Audience

Primary:
- adults
- people in London
- people interested in learning parkour
- people looking for unusual physical training
- people looking for challenging fitness/movement activities

Do not assume that the word "parkour" is always the best marketing
positioning. Test alternatives such as practical movement where
appropriate.

## Geography

London is the primary market.

Local intent is highly important.

## Business positioning

LondonParkour should feel:
- professional
- serious
- challenging
- welcoming
- intelligent
- skill-focused

Avoid:
- childish presentation
- "ninja" imagery/language
- military/tactical positioning
- extreme-risk positioning
- generic fitness clichés

## Conversion hierarchy

Primary:
- completed paid booking

Secondary:
- checkout started
- enquiry

Micro conversions:
- class page visit
- pricing page visit
- engagement

Never treat micro conversions as equivalent to bookings.

## Advertising principles

Do not recommend changes simply because Google recommends them.

Every recommendation should have:
1. evidence
2. hypothesis
3. expected benefit
4. potential downside
5. measurement method

Never make changes to production Google Ads without explicit approval.

## AI behaviour

Separate:
- observed facts
- interpretation
- hypothesis
- recommendation

Never invent performance data.

When data is insufficient, say so.

Prefer controlled experiments over simultaneous changes.
